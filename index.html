How to read this site

Every page here comes in two forms, made from the same text. Use whichever suits you:

  • In any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari…): you're probably doing that now. Pages end in .html.
  • In a Markdown browser such as BonBon: open https://ludwa6.github.io/index.md. Pages end in .md, and you choose how they look.
  • In a feed reader (FeedLand, NetNewsWire, rss.chat…): subscribe to https://ludwa6.github.io/rss.xml for every new post, in full.
  • As plain text, for scripts and AI tools: the .md pages, listed in llms.txt.

No tracking, no scripts, no account needed.
